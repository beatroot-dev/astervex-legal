# Astervex Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 4, 2026

Astervex is developed and published by Dmitrii Kryzhanovskii. Beatroot Development is the project and brand name used for Astervex. This policy explains what data Astervex stores and how that data is used.

Contact: beat.root.dev@icloud.com

## Data Astervex Stores

Astervex stores the following data to provide the game:

- Account data: Astervex profile ID, internal account identifiers, hashed password verifier data, active session metadata, and device/session identifiers used to sign in and prevent account conflicts across devices.
- Gameplay data: player profile, pilot nickname, selected character, high score, games played, balances, unlocked content, inventory, ship configuration, map progress, and related saved-game state.
- Purchase delivery data: StoreKit transaction identifiers, original transaction identifiers, product identifiers, crystal amount, purchase date, and delivery date. This is used to deliver in-app purchases and prevent duplicate delivery.

Astervex does not ask for real name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health data, or payment card details.

## How Astervex Uses Data

Astervex uses the stored data only for app functionality:

- creating and signing in to an Astervex game account;
- syncing saved-game progress with iCloud through Apple CloudKit;
- keeping one active account session consistent across devices;
- saving gameplay progress, balances, inventory, and unlocked content;
- delivering StoreKit in-app purchases and preventing duplicate crystal delivery.

Astervex does not use this data for third-party advertising, developer advertising, data broker sharing, or cross-app tracking.

## iCloud and CloudKit

Astervex uses Apple CloudKit to store game account, gameplay, and purchase delivery records in the user's private iCloud database. This lets the same Astervex account and progress work across the user's devices signed in to iCloud.

Data stored or processed by Apple services is also subject to Apple's privacy policies and iCloud terms.

## In-App Purchases

Astervex uses Apple's StoreKit for in-app purchases. Apple processes payment and App Store transaction infrastructure. Astervex stores limited purchase delivery records, such as transaction identifiers and product identifiers, so purchased crystals can be delivered once and not duplicated.

Astervex does not store payment card numbers or other payment method details.

## Account Deletion

When account deletion succeeds in the app, Astervex deletes the app's game account record, player profile record, known purchase delivery ledger records for that account, and local cached account/profile data from the device.

Deleting an Astervex account does not delete App Store purchase history, StoreKit transaction records, iCloud backups, or other records controlled by Apple.

## Children

Astervex is not designed to request personal contact information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through Astervex, contact beat.root.dev@icloud.com.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when Astervex changes its data practices. The effective date above will be updated when the policy changes.
