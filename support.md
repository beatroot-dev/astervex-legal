# Astervex Support

If you need help with Astervex, contact us at [beat.root.dev@icloud.com](mailto:beat.root.dev@icloud.com).

## What we can help with

- Account creation, sign-in, and active sessions
- iCloud synchronization and saved progress
- In-app purchase delivery
- Gameplay questions
- Bugs, crashes, and other technical issues

## What to include in your message

To help us investigate, please include:

- Your device model
- Your iOS or iPadOS version
- Your Astervex version
- A description of the issue and the steps that led to it
- A screenshot, if it does not contain private information

Do not send your Astervex password, Apple ID password, payment-card details, or other sensitive information.

## Account deletion

You can delete an Astervex account inside the app. Open **Settings → Accounts**, select the account, and choose **Delete Account**.

For details about data handling, see the [Astervex Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.html).
